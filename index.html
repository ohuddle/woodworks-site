Questions

Common questions.

Do you supply the kitchen?

Most of our customers choose a Howdens kitchen, and we can arrange a free Howdens survey to measure up and plan the layout. If you've already bought a kitchen from another supplier, such as Magnet, Wren, Wickes or B&Q, we can fit that too.

Is the survey free?

Yes, for Howdens kitchens. For kitchens from other suppliers, get in touch and we'll talk you through the options.

How long does a kitchen fit take?

It depends on the size of the kitchen and how much work is involved. You'll get a clear timescale with your written quote, so you know what to expect before we start.

Can you do the plastering and decorating too?

Yes. Once the kitchen is fitted we can make good and plaster the walls, paint the room and fit new skirting and architrave. We offer this for kitchens only.

Do you take the old kitchen away?

We can strip out your old kitchen, but we don't offer rubbish clearance. You'll need a skip or a waste removal service for the old units.

Are you insured?

Yes, we're fully insured.

Which areas do you cover?

Hillingdon and the surrounding areas, including Ruislip, Eastcote, Ickenham, Uxbridge, Northwood, Harefield, Pinner, Northolt, Harrow, Gerrards Cross and the Chalfonts.
