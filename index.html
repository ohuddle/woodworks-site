How it works

From first call to finished kitchen.

STEP 1

Survey

Choosing a Howdens kitchen? We can arrange a free home survey to measure up and plan the layout. For other suppliers, we'll talk you through the options.

STEP 2

Fixed written quote

An itemised price within a few days, with what's included and what isn't spelled out clearly.

STEP 3

Fitting

A start date that suits you, dust sheets down, a tidy site at the end of each day, and a daily update on progress.

STEP 4

Final walk-round

We go through every door, drawer and appliance with you before we call it finished.

About WoodWorks

A carpenter and joiner first, which is why the details line up.

WoodWorks is run by Wayne, a carpenter and joiner with more than 30 years in the trade. After years of fitted furniture, flooring and joinery, the business now does one thing: kitchens. That background shows in the parts most fitters rush, like scribing to uneven walls, level runs of units and tight worktop joints.

You deal with Wayne from the first visit to the final walk-round. No sales team and no subcontracted fitters you've never met.
