Privacy

This page explains what happens to the details you send through this website. WoodWorks Services is run by Wayne Huddle, a sole trader based in Ruislip.

What we collect

When you use the enquiry form, we receive the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, postcode, information about your kitchen and any photos you attach.

How we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry, arrange a survey and quote for your job. We don't sell your details or use them for marketing.

Where it's kept

Enquiries are delivered by our form provider, Formspree, and stored in our email inbox. We delete enquiries that don't lead to work after 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time. Email hello@woodworksservices.com or call 07931 979003. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
